The short version

Peelworks does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers or social media pixels. We only store what's needed to make the shop work.

What we store

Instead of cookies, the site uses your browser's local storage for two things:

NameWhat it doesHow long
peelworks-cartRemembers what's in your cart, so it's still there if you leave and come back.Until you check out or clear it
pw-modelRemembers the phone model you picked.Until you clear it
pw-saved, pw-recentRemember the designs you saved and recently looked at.Until you clear them
pw-token, pw-userOnly if you sign in: keeps you signed in and shows your initial in the menu.Until you sign out, or 60 days

This information stays on your device. Your cart is only sent to us when you check out, and your sign-in token is only sent to our server to show your account and orders.

Third-party services

  • Stripe may set cookies on the checkout page to process your payment securely and prevent fraud. These are strictly necessary for paying. See Stripe's cookie policy.
  • Cloudflare may set a security cookie to protect the site from bots and attacks.
  • Google Fonts provides the site's typeface. Your browser contacts Google to download it.

Your choices

You can clear local storage and cookies at any time in your browser settings. If you do, your cart will be emptied. Blocking cookies entirely may stop the Stripe checkout from working.

Changes

If we ever add analytics or other tools, we'll update this page first and, where required, ask for your consent.
