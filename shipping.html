Processing time

Every skin and sticker is printed, laminated and cut by hand after you order.

  • Designs from our collection: usually 2–4 business days to make.
  • Custom photo skins: usually 3–5 business days to make.
  • Orders placed on weekends or holidays start processing the next business day.

Shipping & delivery

We currently ship to United States addresses only.

  • Cost: free on every US order.
  • Carrier: USPS, packed flat with a stiff backer so nothing bends.
  • Delivery: usually 3–8 business days after your order ships.

Delivery times are estimates from the carrier and aren't guaranteed.

Wrong address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email jatinkumarpyara@gmail.com right away. We can fix it if the order hasn't shipped yet. We can't be responsible for orders sent to an address entered incorrectly.

Lost or late packages

If your order hasn't arrived within 15 business days of shipping, email jatinkumarpyara@gmail.com. We'll check with USPS and, if it's lost, send a free replacement or a refund.

If tracking shows "delivered" but you can't find it, check with neighbours and your local post office first, then let us know.

Returns

Not happy with an item? You can return unused, unapplied designs from our collection within 30 days of delivery. See the Refund policy for the full details, including what can't be returned (such as applied skins and custom photo skins).

Damaged, misprinted or wrong item

If anything arrives damaged, misprinted or isn't what you ordered, email jatinkumarpyara@gmail.com within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement or a full refund, your choice, and you won't need to send anything back.
