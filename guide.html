Application guide

Two minutes.Zero bubbles.

Everything you need to put your skin on perfectly the first time, plus how to take it off and keep it looking new.

You’ll needYour skinAn alcohol wipe or lint-free clothA card (credit card or gift card)A hair dryer (optional)
Before you start: work somewhere clean and not too cold. Skins grip best at room temperature, and a bathroom after a hot shower has less dust in the air.
Step 1

Clean the back

Wipe the back of your phone with an alcohol wipe or a lint-free cloth. Any dust or fingerprint left under the skin shows as a bump.

Step 2

Peel the top only

Lift the backing from the top edge of the big back panel, about an inch. Don’t peel the whole thing off yet, so the adhesive stays clean.

Step 3

Line up the camera

Match the cut-out with the camera bump first. That’s your anchor. Lightly tack the top edge down; you can lift and reposition it while it’s only touching lightly.

Step 4

Smooth top to bottom

Slowly pull the backing away from underneath while pressing the skin down with a card, working from the middle out to the edges. Slow beats fast.

Step 5

Add the camera piece

Peel the small camera piece and line each hole up with a lens. Press it down around every lens with a fingertip.

Step 6

Seal the edges

Run your finger firmly around every edge and corner. A few seconds of warm air from a hair dryer on low helps the corners grip; keep it moving and don’t overheat.

Got a bubble?

  • Right after applying: lift the skin gently back to the bubble and lay it down again with the card.
  • Small bubbles usually work out on their own within a day or two.
  • Stubborn bubble: prick it with a pin and press the air out with your finger.

Taking it off

  • Warm the skin for a few seconds with a hair dryer on low.
  • Start at a corner and peel slowly, at a low angle, close to the phone.
  • Any sticky residue wipes off with an alcohol wipe.

Keep it looking new

  • Give the adhesive 24 hours to fully set before heavy use.
  • Clean it with a slightly damp cloth. Skip harsh cleaners.
  • Avoid leaving your phone in direct sun on a hot dashboard.
