What can be returned

You can return items from our design collection for a refund within 30 days of delivery if they are:

  • unused and not applied to a device,
  • still on their original backing sheet, and
  • in the condition you received them.

What can't be returned

  • Applied skins or stickers. Once the adhesive has been used, we can't resell or reuse the item.
  • Custom photo skins. They're made just for you from your own picture, so they can't be returned unless they arrive damaged or misprinted.
  • Items ordered for the wrong phone model. Please double-check your model before ordering. Email us before your order ships and we'll happily switch it.

Damaged or misprinted items

Mistakes happen. If an item arrives damaged, misprinted, cut wrong, or isn't what you ordered, including custom photo skins, email jatinkumarpyara@gmail.com within 14 days of delivery with a photo. We'll send a free replacement or a full refund. You don't need to return the faulty item.

How to start a return

Email jatinkumarpyara@gmail.com with your order number and the items you'd like to return. We'll reply with the return address. Please send items back flat, in a stiff envelope, so they arrive undamaged.

Unless the item was faulty, return shipping is paid by you. We recommend a tracked service, since we can't refund returns that never reach us.

Refunds

Once we receive and check your return, we'll refund the item price to your original payment method. Stripe processes the refund straight away. Depending on your bank, it can take 5–10 business days to show on your statement.

Shipping is free, so there are no shipping costs to refund or deduct.

Cancelling an order

Changed your mind? Email jatinkumarpyara@gmail.com as soon as possible. If we haven't started printing your order, we'll cancel it and refund you in full. Once an order has been printed, it can no longer be cancelled, but eligible items can still be returned as above.

Before you dispute a charge

If something is wrong with your order, please contact us first at jatinkumarpyara@gmail.com. We're a small shop and will always try to make it right. Disputes filed with your bank take much longer to resolve than a refund from us.
