Who we are

Peelworks is a small online shop based in Washington, USA, selling hand-printed phone skins and vinyl stickers at peel-work.com. In this policy, "we", "us" and "Peelworks" mean the shop and its owner. Questions about your privacy can be sent to jatinkumarpyara@gmail.com.

Information we collect

When you place an order, we receive:

  • your name, email address and shipping address,
  • what you ordered, including the phone model you chose, and
  • the order total and payment status.

When you create an account, we store your name, email address, a securely hashed version of your password (we can't read your actual password), whether you've confirmed your email, and a list of your orders and their shipping status. Signing in saves a sign-in token in your browser so you stay signed in for up to 60 days.

When you make a custom photo skin, we receive the photo you upload, along with how you zoomed and positioned it.

When you browse the site, your cart and preferred phone model are saved in your own browser (see the Cookie policy). Our hosting providers may also log basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type to keep the site running and secure.

Card details: we never see or store your card number. Payments are entered directly into a secure form provided by Stripe.

How we use it

  • to make, ship and deliver your order,
  • to run your account: signing you in, sending email confirmation and password reset codes, and showing your order history and tracking,
  • to email you about your order, such as a receipt, a question about your custom photo, or a shipping problem,
  • to handle returns, refunds and customer support,
  • to prevent fraud and keep the site secure, and
  • to keep the records we're required to keep for taxes and accounting.

We don't sell your information, and we don't send marketing emails unless you've asked to receive them.

Who we share it with

We only share information with the services that help us run the shop:

  • Stripe: processes payments and collects your shipping address. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • USPS: receives your name and address to deliver your order.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare: host the website and protect it from attacks.
  • Google: if we use Google Sheets to organise orders, and to load the site's fonts.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

Your custom photos

Photos you upload are stored privately on our own server and are used only to print your skin. We don't share them, post them, or use them for advertising without your permission. We delete uploaded photos within 90 days after your order is completed, and photos from orders that were never paid are deleted regularly.

How long we keep information

We keep order records, including name, address and items, for as long as we need them for taxes, accounting, returns and warranty questions, generally up to 7 years. Custom photos are kept for up to 90 days as described above. If you have an account, we keep it until you ask us to delete it; sign-in tokens expire after 60 days, and email codes expire after 15 minutes.

Your choices and rights

You can see your orders any time on your account page. You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your account or the personal information we hold about you by emailing jatinkumarpyara@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days. We may need to keep some records, such as order details, where the law requires it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws, and we'll honour those requests too.

Security

We use secure (HTTPS) connections across the site, keep payment handling with Stripe, store passwords only as salted hashes, and limit access to order information to the people running the shop. No method of storing information online is completely secure, but we work to protect yours.

Children

Peelworks isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us their information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
