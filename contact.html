Help with an order

Email jatinkumarpyara@gmail.com with your order number (it's in your Stripe receipt email), the email you ordered with, and a short description of the issue.

For a damaged or misprinted item, please attach a photo. It lets us send a replacement right away.

Custom photo skins

If your uploaded photo looks low quality or doesn't fit well, we may email you before printing to suggest a better crop or a sharper image.

Want to change your photo after ordering? Email us as soon as possible at jatinkumarpyara@gmail.com. We can usually swap it if the order hasn't been printed yet.

Clubs, events and bulk orders

Making stickers or skins for a student club, team or event? Email jatinkumarpyara@gmail.com with roughly how many you need and your deadline, and we'll get back to you with options.

Response times

Peelworks is a small studio, so we answer email on weekdays and usually reply within 1–2 business days. Orders keep shipping as normal.
